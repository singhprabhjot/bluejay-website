Box Truck Helper

Fremont, CA · Full-time / Part-time

$22.00/hr 18+ welcome Paid training Benefits + PTO

18 or older? You can apply — no experience needed. We train you to be skilled as the best of the best in the business. Box truck helpers start at $22.00/hr, with room to grow.

Bluejay Delivery is actively hiring full-time and part-time driver helpers to deliver 30–70 bulkier, heavier Amazon packages in branded box trucks out of our Fremont, CA warehouse. Join one of the top-performing Amazon DSPs in North America.

Hours & Compensation

  • Starting at $22.00 per hour
  • Shifts available around the clock — your schedule is confirmed during hiring
  • 8 and 10 hour shifts; full-time hours with at least one weekend day
  • $125 Zappos credit for work shoes
  • PTO, health insurance, overtime as needed, bonuses, and advancement opportunities

Qualifications

  • At least 18 years old with a valid state ID
  • Able to exit and enter the vehicle 40+ times daily, use stairs, and lift up to 90 lbs solo or 300 lbs with a team lift
  • Willing to complete a drug test and background check
  • Attention to detail and stellar customer service through an 8+ hour day

Benefits

  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • 401(k) with company matching
  • Paid time off and paid training
  • Flexible schedule

Work location: In person — Fremont, CA
