DOT Box Truck Driver

Tracy, CA (near Lathrop & Stockton) · Full-time · Non-CDL · Dock-to-dock

$27.75/hr 8–10 hr shifts Non-CDL Benefits + PTO

Drive a non-CDL box truck dock to dock. All trucks come with an electric lift gate and industrial hand truck/dolly. We start early and finish early — great for those who enjoy being home early. Must be polite and professional; customer satisfaction is a must.

Requirements

  • No CDL required — a regular Class C license is fine
  • At least 23 years old
  • 1+ year of box truck experience
  • Load box trucks; shift starts between 10 AM and 2 AM, 7–9 hour shift
  • One weekend day per week required
  • Follow strict safety standards on and off the road
  • Operate an electronic device for GPS and daily records
  • Excellent attendance record, dedication, and willingness to learn
  • Complete a full background check

Pay & Schedule

  • Full-time at $27.75 per hour
  • 8 and 10 hour shifts, including night shifts
  • Weekends as needed

Benefits

  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • 401(k)
  • Paid time off and paid training
  • Flexible schedule

Work location: On the road / In person — Tracy, CA

Nearby cities

We're conveniently located for candidates in and around Tracy and the greater San Joaquin Valley, including Lathrop, Manteca, Stockton, Ripon, French Camp, Mountain House, Lodi, Modesto, Livermore, and Pleasanton.
