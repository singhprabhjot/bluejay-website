DOT Box Truck Driver

Fremont, CA · Full-time / Part-time

$25.50/hr No CDL required Paid training Benefits + PTO

We train you to be skilled as the best of the best in the business. Certified drivers start at $25.50/hr — grow with us immediately.

Bluejay Delivery is actively hiring full-time and part-time delivery drivers to deliver 30–70 bulkier, heavier packages in box trucks out of Fremont, CA. Fewer packages means higher attention to detail and stellar customer service, while still achieving an 8+ hour day.

Hours & Compensation

  • Certified drivers start at $25.50 per hour
  • Make it home for dinner — shifts begin at 10:00 AM, day shifts only, no nights
  • Full-time hours; one weekend day required
  • Monday to Friday, with weekends as needed
  • $125 Zappos credit for work shoes
  • Advancement opportunities: Lead Driver, Dispatcher, Operations Manager

Qualifications

  • At least 23 years old
  • Licensed for 3+ years — no CDL required
  • Able to lift and maneuver up to 70 lbs solo and up to 300 lbs with a team
  • Willing to complete a drug test and MVR check
  • Comfortable operating GPS on a smartphone

Benefits

  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • 401(k)
  • Company truck
  • Paid time off and paid training
  • Flexible schedule

Work location: On the road / In person — Fremont, CA
